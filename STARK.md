# STARK Digital website conventions

- Present STARK Digital as the portfolio and independent research practice of Jeremiah Wong Zhi Qi.
- Use vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; keep the public site fast and dependency-free.
- Treat `articles/*.md` as canonical research sources and generate crawlable pages in `research/*.html`.
- Keep publication metadata in `data/articles.json` and progress in `data/progress.json`.
- Publish measured failures and research boundaries alongside positive results.
- Do not describe a capability as accepted unless its actual acceptance gate passed.
- Do not describe numerical separation, retrieval, or causal delivery as understanding.
- Founder V0 is the immutable M7 baseline; descendants must be named separately and compared against it.
- Governance remains outside learned state and mutable heredity.
